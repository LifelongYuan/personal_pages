I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mechanical and Automation Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), working with Professor Yunhui Liu in the Legged Robot Lab (CUHKLRL). My research centers on learning-based control for legged robots, with the goal of closing the gap between theory and real-world deployment.

Before joining CUHK, I earned my B.E. in Automation and Control from the Chu Kochen Honors College at Zhejiang University in 2022.

I am broadly interested in robot learning, including:

  • Reinforcement Learning for locomotion, navigation and manipulation.
  • Real-world robot learning.
  • Agentic system for legged robots.
  • Legged Systems (Hoppers, Quadrupeds, and Bipeds)
